Our Mission

Baked with purpose.

Our mission is to create exciting vegan baked goods that not only satisfy your cravings but also contribute towards making a positive impact on the world. As a Washington State Cottage Food approved microbakery, we use our profits from your orders to donate to animal sanctuaries or invest in the growth and development of our business, which in turn brings more satisfied customers and thus more donations to sanctuaries.

Why Mix for a Mission

Small bakery, big heart.

100% Vegan

Entirely plant-based

Every cake, cupcake, cookie, and cake pop is 100% vegan — no dairy, no eggs, no compromise on flavor.

Cottage Food

Washington State approved

We operate as a registered Cottage Food microbakery out of our Kirkland home kitchen, inspected and approved by Washington State.

Gives Back

Profits with a purpose

We've donated over $1,000 to animal sanctuaries and charities, and donate 50% of profits to Hope Link to support local low-income families.

Outreach & Partnerships

Giving beyond the kitchen.

We take our treats into the community. From celebrating Indian heritage at Eastside Dandiya to supporting Seattle Children's Hospital with Wave Seattle, we look for every chance to turn baking into impact.

  • $1,000+ donated to animal sanctuaries and charities to date.
  • 50% of profits go to Hope Link, supporting local low-income families.
  • Partnered with Wave Seattle, which has raised $15,000+ for Seattle Children's Hospital.
