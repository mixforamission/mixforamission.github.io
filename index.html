About Us

Baked with purpose.

Mix for a Mission is a Washington State Cottage Food approved vegan home microbakery, founded by high school students. We create exciting vegan baked goods that satisfy your cravings — and use our profits to donate to animal sanctuaries and invest in our growth, so every order brings more donations to the causes we care about.
