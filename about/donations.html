Donations

Where your order goes.

Mix for a Mission has donated over $1,000 to date. We donate 50% of profits from every order to Hope Link, a local organization supporting low-income families in our community, and put the rest toward animal sanctuaries and growing the bakery so we can give more over time.

Our Results

Every order gives back.

Half of every order’s profit goes straight to Hope Link, and the rest funds animal sanctuaries and the bakery’s growth — so each satisfied customer means more donations down the line.
