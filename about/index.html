About Us

Students with a sweet tooth for change.

Mix for a Mission is a vegan home microbakery founded in 2024 by high school students in Kirkland, WA. We bake because we love it — and because every order lets us give back.

Team photo
How it started

From a home kitchen to a mission.

We started Mix for a Mission in 2024 as high school students who wanted to combine two things we care about: baking incredible vegan treats and making a tangible difference for animals and our community.

Operating as a Washington State Cottage Food approved microbakery out of our Kirkland home kitchen means we can keep things small, personal, and intentional — every bake is made to order, by hand.

Our mission

Our mission is to create exciting vegan baked goods that not only satisfy your cravings but also contribute towards making a positive impact on the world. As a Washington State Cottage Food approved microbakery, we use our profits from your orders to donate to animal sanctuaries or invest in the growth and development of our business, which in turn brings more satisfied customers and thus more donations to sanctuaries.

Animal sanctuaries & charities

As a vegan bakery, animal welfare is at the heart of what we do. To date we've donated over $1,000 to animal sanctuaries and charities. The more we grow, the more we can give — which is why we reinvest the rest of our profits into the bakery, bringing in more customers and, in turn, more donations.

Hope Link — local families

We donate 50% of our profits to Hope Link, a local organization supporting low-income families in our community. It's our way of making sure the impact of every order reaches people close to home, too.

Have a question about our donations or charity partnerships? Our treasurer Tandisse is happy to help — get in touch.
