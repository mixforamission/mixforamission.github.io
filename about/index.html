About Us

A vegan home bakery on a mission.

Mix for a Mission is a vegan home bakery in the Greater Seattle Area, Washington founded in 2024 by high school students. We specialize in custom vegan cakes, cookies, cupcakes and cake pops. Each order is always fresh, and created in our home bakery with both pick-up and delivery options available.

Our Mission

Why we bake.

Our mission is to create exciting vegan baked goods that not only satisfy your cravings but also contribute towards making a positive impact on the world. As a Washington State Cottage Food approved microbakery, we use our profits from your orders to donate to animal sanctuaries or invest in the growth and development of our business, which in turn brings more satisfied customers and thus more donations to sanctuaries.

Our Results

Every order gives back.

Since 2024, your orders have helped us donate over $1,000 to animal sanctuaries and the causes we care about. We reinvest the rest into growing the bakery — so every satisfied customer means more donations down the line.
