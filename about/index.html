About Us

A vegan home bakery on a mission.

Mix for a Mission is a vegan home bakery in Kirkland, Washington, founded in 2024 by high school students. We make custom vegan cakes, cookies, cupcakes, and cake pops. Everything is baked fresh to order in our home kitchen, with pickup and delivery available.

Our Mission

Why we bake.

We bake vegan goods and give half the profits to Hope Link, a local organization that supports families in need. The other half goes back into the bakery so we can reach more customers and donate more over time. Simple as that. We're a Washington State Cottage Food approved microbakery — which means everything is made in a home kitchen, not a commercial facility.

Our Results

Every order gives back.

Since 2024, your orders have helped us donate over $1,000 to Hope Link and the causes we care about. We put the rest back into the bakery. More customers means more donations.
