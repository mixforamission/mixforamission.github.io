    Ordering & Policies

    Everything you need to know before you order — how it works, timing, allergies, and add-ons.

    How ordering works

    After you submit the form, I’ll text you back with pickup or delivery instructions, timing, and payment details. No order is confirmed until payment is made.

    Payment is via Venmo, arranged after you submit the form (we don’t collect payment in the form itself). Once paid, all sales are final.

    Rush orders

    Rush orders are welcome, but may be overbooked. If your event is in less than 2 days, text 425-739-9862 for a faster response.

    Typical response time is under 48 hours — if you haven’t heard back by then, follow up by text or email.

    Gluten-free options

    Items marked with * on the menu can be modified gluten-free for an additional $5 (cakes) or $3 (everything else).

    There’s a real chance of trace gluten in the final product despite precautions — not recommended for anyone with celiac disease.

    Nut allergies

    Products are generally nut-free unless nuts are in the name (for example, a peanut butter flavor), but equipment is shared and only washed once between uses, so cross-contamination is possible.

    Allergy note

    All baked goods are made in a shared home kitchen and may contain trace amounts of common allergens. Mix for a Mission is not liable for allergic reactions after products leave the kitchen — if you have severe allergies, please don’t consume or share these products without checking first.

    Customization

    Under Washington’s Cottage Food law, customization is limited. Simple requests (for example, dietary notes) can go in the order form’s comments.

    All customization starts at $60 and increases with complexity — we’re not a professional cake decorator and will do our best within our ability.

    Cake add-ons
    • Happy Birthday topper — +$2. Small gold non-edible decorations.
    • Worst Wishes card — +$5. A humorous birthday card from Worst Wishes LLC, a fellow student-led business. Comes with your order.

    Add either when you order — just note it in the form.
