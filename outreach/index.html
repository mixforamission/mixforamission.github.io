Eastside Dandiya 2024

We went to this event in 2024 to celebrate a blend of our Indian heritage and vegan baked goods! Eastside Dandiya was an amazing event where we got to meet the large Indian vegan community. The Rose and Mango dessert jars were the perfect way to allow us to connect with our community and continue giving back.
