Where we've shown up.

A few of the events and partnerships we're proud to be part of.

2024

Eastside Dandiya

We joined Eastside Dandiya, a celebration of Indian heritage, to share our vegan baked goods with the community. It was a chance to honor culture and introduce more people to plant-based treats — all in one vibrant evening.

Cultural celebration · Vegan baked goods
2024

Wave Seattle — Seattle Children's Hospital

We took part in a Wave Seattle event supporting Seattle Children's Hospital. Wave has raised over $15,000 for the hospital, and we were glad to contribute our vegan bakes to help power that effort.

Hospital support · $15,000+ raised by Wave
Ongoing

Hope Link — supporting local families

Beyond events, 50% of our profits go to Hope Link, a local organization supporting low-income families. This is our everyday commitment — every order you place helps fund it.

Partner spotlight

Why we partner locally.

As a microbakery, we can't change the world on our own — but we can show up for our corner of it. Partnering with local events and organizations lets us multiply the good each order does.

Whether it's celebrating Indian heritage at Eastside Dandiya, helping Wave Seattle raise money for Seattle Children's Hospital, or quietly supporting families through Hope Link, we're always looking for the next chance to turn baking into impact.

Know of a community event or cause that could use some vegan baked goods? Reach out — we'd love to hear about it.
