Our Menu

Everything vegan, baked to order.

Custom cakes, cupcakes, cookies & cake pops — all plant-based, all made fresh in our Kirkland kitchen. Pick a category, choose your flavors, and place an order. Every purchase helps us donate to animal sanctuaries.

Flavors marked with * can be made gluten-free for an additional $5 (cakes) or $3 (everything else) — just note it when you order.

Full ingredient lists are available for select items — tap Ingredients & Details on any item. Everything is vegan & eggless. All items are made in a shared kitchen and may contain traces of common allergens.
