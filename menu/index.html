Our Menu

Everything vegan, baked to order.

Custom cakes, cupcakes and cookies. All plant-based, all made to order in our Kirkland kitchen. Pick a category, choose your flavors, and place an order. Every purchase helps us donate to animal sanctuaries.

Flavors marked with * can be made gluten-free for an additional $5 (cakes) or $3 (everything else) — just note it when you order.

Tap any item to see its full details: ingredients, allergens, sizing, and more. Everything is vegan and eggless. All items are made in a shared kitchen and may contain traces of common allergens.
